What are you doing when you log 10 pushups: running, fending him off, or paying?

Three metaphors for the same chase, each tried against three pursuers. Pay is what the mocks have used so far. Step counts use the Outrun rule from the last mock. Pursuer names, props, and lines are placeholders.

Also considered: a pursuer that is weather or terrain, such as a tide, lava, or nightfall (nobody to like or fear), and feeding the pursuer every day (the same story as paying).

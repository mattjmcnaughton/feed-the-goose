What gets you to pay: a chase, The Man's attitude, or a receipt?

Three directions for Pay the Man. Each opens part paid, so pay off the rest to see its paid-in-full moment. In The Chase, drag the time of day. The name, URL, and history are sample data.

Also considered: a cash register with chunky keys and a drawer that flies open (tactile, but two taps per payment and the most UI to build), a slot machine (luck is the wrong reward for paying dues), and progress rings (Apple Fitness already owns them).

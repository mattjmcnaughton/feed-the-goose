Three takes on each goose moment. Pick one per moment and it plays; replay it, drop to quarter speed, or drag the frame slider to step through it. It opens on my picks. Step counts use the Outrun rule at 6 pm; the art is still a placeholder.

Every clip poses the same jointed goose (neck, head, beak, wing, legs, belly, eyes) over time, so the picks can be rebuilt in SwiftUI or Rive from the same joints. With Reduce Motion on, each moment jumps to its last frame. Also considered, not drawn: a warning honk when the goose gets within 10 steps, and sound and haptics, which a browser mock cannot show.
